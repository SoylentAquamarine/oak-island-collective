## Evidence and falsification rules

Distinguish original issue, facsimile, transcription, catalogue metadata, later reproduction, and search-synthesis. Catalogue coverage cannot prove an issue survives; a reproduced quotation cannot be promoted to a verified cipher. If the primary record cannot establish a stable inscription/transcription, stop cryptanalysis rather than optimize a speculative text.

## Wasted effort, blockers, and compute

Broad web searching has diminishing returns now that the exact UNB title and microfilm contact are known. The blocker is issue-level access and original-page retrieval, not compute. No cryptanalytic or statistical run is justified.

## Ethics and permissions
