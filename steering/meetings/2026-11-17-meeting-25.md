## Evidence reviewed — new archival locator
The UNB New Brunswick Historical Newspapers Project identifies Daily News (Amherst, Nova Scotia) as published 2 Nov 1895–11 Mar 1914. It lists public microform holdings at Harriet Irving Library, filed as Amherst Daily News (HIL-MIC). The target 23 Feb 1912 date falls within that catalogued run. The later title Amherst Daily News (1914) begins 12 Mar 1914, so the 1912 issue should be requested under the earlier Daily News title/filed-as record. UNB lists Microforms staff at mic@unb.ca and provides external access to microfilm readers/scanners. Nova Scotia Archives warns that its online microfilm database is not a complete index of all surviving newspaper holdings; its 1976 annual report historically records Amherst Daily News microfilm for 1895–1974, but that does not establish current reel availability.

The 1973 M.R. Chappell manuscript still supplies only a retrospective six-point account attributed to Frederick Blair's 23 Feb 1912 reply (pp. 44–46). No original 1912 page was found or read.

## Falsification and uncertainty
A catalogued date range does not prove the specific issue survives or that Chappell transcribed it accurately. The 1973 manuscript is not the 1912 newspaper. None of this authenticates the lost stone or inscription. The 1911 Bowdoin article's date discrepancy (11 vs 19 August) remains a separate source-collation question.
