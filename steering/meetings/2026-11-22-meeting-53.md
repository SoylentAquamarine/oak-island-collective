# Steering Committee Meeting #53 — 2026-11-22 13:53 ET

**Project goal.** Establish what is actually documented about the alleged 90-foot stone and the later cipher story. Cryptanalysis remains conditional on authenticating a primary inscription or a defensible contemporary transcription.

## Evidence reviewed
- The official Nova Scotia Archives newspaper database lists Amherst Daily News reel 2634, covering 25 November 1911 through 29 June 1912; this includes the alleged 23 February 1912 Blair-reply date.
- The Archives' newspaper-microfilm guide says its listed films are not available through interlibrary loan; researchers must visit the Halifax Archives or hire a researcher.
- UNB independently lists title-level Daily News holdings from 2 November 1895 through 11 March 1914.
- None of these catalogue records proves the exact 23-February issue survives, is legible, or contains Blair's reply. No original issue was inspected.

## Evidence standard and falsification
Require the original issue/page, exact date/title/page/column, full context and a reproducible transcription. If the issue is absent or the alleged reply is not present, retire that lead. Secondary retellings do not establish the stone's disappearance date or a genuine inscription.

## Wasted effort, blockers and capacity
Broad web searching has diminishing returns. OCR, cryptanalysis and compute are premature without a primary text. The immediate resource is issue-level archive access, not a solver.

## Ethics and access
Respect archive access, copying and publication terms. No archive contact, paid researcher or reproduction request was made; that requires user authorization.
