## Falsification and uncertainty
The 1973 manuscript's selected passages may be described as “quoting verbatim,” but the six-point summary is not independently verified against the 1912 newspaper. It cannot establish the stone's authenticity or inscription. The lost stone prevents physical inspection. If no independently sourced inscription image, rubbing or reliable transcription survives, the defensible endpoint may be a provenance history rather than cryptanalysis.
