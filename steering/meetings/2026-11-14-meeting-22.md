## Falsification and uncertainty
A negative search of one issue cannot prove the stone never existed. A compiled transcription cannot be upgraded to an original scan or eyewitness account without provenance. Keep the Colonist chapter separate from the Cooke correspondence. A valid endpoint may be that no genuine cipher can be verified.
