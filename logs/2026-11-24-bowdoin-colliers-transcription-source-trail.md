# Bowdoin Collier's transcription source trail — 2026-11-24

## Purpose
Record a newly located text-transcription lead without promoting it to primary-source status or treating it as independent confirmation of the Oak Island stone claim.

## Sources and evidence tiers
1. **UNB catalogue (institutional holdings record):** [Daily News (Amherst, Nova Scotia)](https://newspapers.lib.unb.ca/newspaper/daily-news-amherst-nova-scotia) lists public HIL-MIC holdings from 1895-11-02 through 1914-03-11. This range includes 1912-02-23, but does not establish that the exact issue survives or that it contains Blair's reply.
2. **Secondary text transcription:** [Jason Colavito, “Solving Oak Island”](https://www.jasoncolavito.com/solving-oak-island.html) reproduces H. L. Bowdoin's “Solving the Mystery of Oak Island,” identifies the author/date as 19 August 1911, and cites *Collier's*, pp. 19–20. The page supplies searchable text, not the original magazine scan.
3. **Secondary summary:** [The Curse of Oak Island — Bowdoin and the Old Gold Salvage Company](https://thecurseofoakisland.com/hunt/captain-henry-bowdoin-and-the-old-gold-salvage-company) summarizes the 1912-02-23 Blair reply and cites M. R. Chappell's unpublished 1973 manuscript. It is not the Amherst Daily News issue or Blair's original text.

## What this does and does not establish
- It provides a specific title/date/page target for locating Bowdoin's original 1911 Collier's article.
- It does not independently authenticate the text transcription against the original issue.
- It does not verify the 1912-02-23 issue, Blair's reply, the alleged stone inscription, or the stone's disappearance date.
- No archive contact was sent, no issue was downloaded, and no cryptanalysis was performed.
