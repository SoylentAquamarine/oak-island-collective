# UNB microfilm access route — 2026-11-23

## Official holdings evidence
UNB's New Brunswick Historical Newspapers Project lists *Daily News (Amherst, Nova Scotia)* from 2 November 1895 through 11 March 1914, with UNB Libraries, Fredericton holding the title on microform:
https://newspapers.lib.unb.ca/newspaper/daily-news-amherst-nova-scotia

That title-level range includes the alleged 23 February 1912 date. It does not prove that the exact issue survives, is legible, or contains Blair's reply.

## Access procedure
UNB's Microforms FAQ says help with microfilm/older back issues is available through Microforms Collections and appointments are required:
https://lib.unb.ca/microforms/newspapers-frequently-asked-questions

The catalogue page lists `mic@unb.ca` and recommends appointments. No inquiry was sent, no issue was viewed, and no reproduction was requested.

## Evidence limits
