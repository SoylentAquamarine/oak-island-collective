# Bowdoin 1911 Collier's transcription: source-tier audit

Date: 2026-11-25.

## Source read
Jason Colavito hosts the complete text attributed to H. L. Bowdoin, “Solving the Mystery of Oak Island,” Collier's, 19 August 1911, pp. 19–20: https://www.jasoncolavito.com/solving-oak-island.html . The article body is presented on the page at web lines 697–795.

The transcription states that Bowdoin examined the stone at Creighton's Halifax bookbindery during the 1909 expedition and described the rock as “of a basalt type, hard and fine-grained.” It reproduces his six conclusions, including his assertion that no system of characters was carved on the stone.

## Evidence tier and limitations
This is a modern web transcription of a primary article, not an inspected original Collier's issue or page image. The host page's date and pagination are source metadata, not an independently authenticated scan. The transcription has not yet been compared line-by-line against an original facsimile. Chappell's 1973 manuscript remains a separate secondary witness and contains a conflicting date variant. The 23-Feb-1912 Amherst issue and Blair's reply remain unverified.
