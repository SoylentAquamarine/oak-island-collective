# Oak Island cycle #128: Bowdoin/Blair source locator and image warning

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Scope:** Text and catalogue audit only. No original newspaper or magazine image was accessed.

## Evidence checked
1. M. R. Chappell, *The True Story of Oak Island* (manuscript dated 20 September 1973), pp. 44–46, hosted as a PDF at [oakislandmystery.com](https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf). The text says Bowdoin's Collier's article appeared in an “August 11, 1911” issue and attributes a six-point Blair reply to the Amherst *Daily News* of 23 February 1912. This is a later manuscript transcription, not either original issue.
2. The Archive.org catalogue locator for the Collier's issue is [sim_colliers-the-national-weekly_1911-08-19_47_22](https://archive.org/details/sim_colliers-the-national-weekly_1911-08-19_47_22/sim_colliers-the-national-weekly_1911-08-19_47_22). A linked page identifies the date as 19 August 1911. Opening the Archive.org item timed out in this session; the facsimile was not inspected.
3. The [UNB Daily News (Amherst) catalogue](https://newspapers.lib.unb.ca/newspaper/daily-news-amherst-nova-scotia) lists title-level microform holdings from 2 November 1895 to 11 March 1914. This does not establish that the individual 23 February 1912 issue survives.
4. A page at [thecurseofoakisland.com](https://thecurseofoakisland.com/research/collier-s-solving-the-mystery-of-oak-island) explicitly labels its hero image an “AI-assisted historical reconstruction, not a photograph.” Do not cite that image as a scan or as primary-source evidence.

## Finding and limits
The 11 August date in Chappell conflicts with the 19 August date in the Archive.org catalogue and other hosted transcriptions. The discrepancy remains unresolved. Chappell's six Bowdoin statements and six-point Blair response are useful locators and witnesses to a later transcription, not substitutes for the 1911 and 1912 originals.
