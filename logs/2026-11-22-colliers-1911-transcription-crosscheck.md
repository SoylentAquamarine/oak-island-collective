## What the transcription says
The full transcription identifies Bowdoin as author and dates the article 19 August 1911. In the article he reports examining the stone in Halifax in 1909 at a bookbindery; he describes it as hard, fine-grained basalt. In his sixth conclusion he says there was no system of characters on the stone, only scratches attributed to employees.
