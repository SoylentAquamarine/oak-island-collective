# Chappell's 1973 manuscript: Bowdoin date and Blair-reply transcription audit

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Scope:** Text-only reading of a hosted PDF transcription. No 1911 magazine scan or 1912 newspaper issue was accessed.

## Source inspected
M. R. Chappell, *The True Story of Oak Island*, a manuscript accompanied by a letter dated 20 September 1973 to Dan Blankenship. The hosted PDF is available at:

https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf

The relevant chapter is headed “Captain Henry L. Bowdoin” (PDF text lines/pages marked 42–46). Chappell states that Bowdoin's Collier's article appeared in the **11 August 1911** issue. He then reproduces six statements attributed to Bowdoin and says Frederick Blair published a categorical reply in the Amherst *Daily News* on **23 February 1912**. Chappell introduces the following six-point response as quoted “verbatim,” including Blair's objections about the condition/alignment of the pit, Smith's Cove drains and water flow, chain-link evidence, parchment, and the inscribed stone.

## Date discrepancy
The date in Chappell's 1973 manuscript conflicts with:
- the Internet Archive catalogue record for *Collier's*, volume 47, number 22, dated **19 August 1911**; and
- Jason Colavito's hosted full transcription, which also cites *Collier's*, **19 August 1911**, pages 19–20: https://www.jasoncolavito.com/solving-oak-island.html

The original magazine pages have not been inspected in this audit. Do not silently resolve the 11-versus-19 August discrepancy. A catalogue record and later transcriptions are locators, not a primary-facsimile reading.

## Evidentiary tier and limits
This is a **1973 manuscript transcription/summary**, not the 1911 Collier's article or the 1912 Amherst newspaper. The manuscript is useful because it preserves a detailed lead to Blair's six-point reply and gives a page-range locator, but the wording and date require comparison with the original issues. The statement that Blair's response was published on 23 February 1912 remains unverified at issue level.

UNB's title catalogue lists Amherst *Daily News* holdings from 2 November 1895 through 11 March 1914. That range includes the target date but does not establish that the specific 23 February 1912 issue survives.

## Research significance
The manuscript adds a second later witness to the Bowdoin/Blair chronology and identifies content to check in the original 1912 reply. It does not independently establish that Blair's words are verbatim, that the 1912 issue survives, or that either party's claims about the stone are correct.
