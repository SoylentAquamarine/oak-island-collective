# SQ-1 — archival-request refinement from a note in the 1864 newspaper compilation

**Trigger:** The current archive-request draft names the 2 January and 27 January 1864 Hunter-Duvar/Cooke correspondence. A closer read of the introductory note in a previously catalogued 2014 newspaper compilation surfaced a separate, more precise archival target.

## Evidence

The PDF *Early Oak Island Documents, Part 1 of 3 — Early Newspaper Articles*, compiled by Les MacPhie (May 2014), lists *The Colonist*'s “History of the Oak Island Enterprise” chapters dated 2 January and 7 January 1864. Its note 1 states that the original handwritten letter dated **27 December 1863** is held by the Nova Scotia Archives and Record Management System and was likely written by Mr. Cooke, secretary to the Oak Island Association.

Source: [PDF compilation](https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/Early%20OI%20Docs%20Part%201%20of%203%20Early%20Newspaper%20Articles.pdf), contents table and note 1. This was directly inspected as a PDF transcription/compilation, not as an archival manuscript. The repository already records the compilation and its 2 January 1864 transcript in `logs/2026-09-28-stone-1864-colonist-account.md`; the specific note about the 27 December 1863 original was not recorded there.

## Interpretation and limits

This is a new **archival locator lead**, not a new primary-source finding. The 27 December 1863 letter's existence, author, archival reference and contents have not been independently verified. It must not be conflated with the separate Hunter-Duvar letter of 2 January 1864 or Cooke reply of 27 January 1864. The compiled newspaper text does not authenticate the lost stone, its alleged inscription, or a cipher.

## Decision

Prepared a new, reviewable request draft that asks the archivist to identify the 27 December 1863 manuscript and separately search for the two January 1864 letters and the related *Colonist* source material. The prior 2026-09-29 log is left unchanged. No request was sent, no manuscript was acquired, and no reproduction permission is inferred.

## Next action

An authorized human sender submits the reviewed request and records the exact fonds/item references, response, access conditions and reproduction terms.
