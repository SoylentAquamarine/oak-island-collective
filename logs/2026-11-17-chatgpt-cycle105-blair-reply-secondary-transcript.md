# ChatGPT cycle 105 — Oak Island Blair-reply source audit

Date: 2026-11-17 19:59 ET.

## Source read
Opened the public M.R. Chappell manuscript PDF dated 20 September 1973:
https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-c/C04%20M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf

PDF pages 44–46 (document lines 782–811) describe Bowdoin's six Collier's claims and preserve a six-point account attributed to Frederick Blair's 23 February 1912 Amherst Daily News reply. The points address shaft alignment, Smith's Cove works, water inflow, the ring bolt, chain links and the stone. Blair reportedly admits that the chain-link claim is based on tradition. This is a retrospective manuscript transcription/summary, not a direct read of the 1912 newspaper.

## Source discrepancy
The 1973 manuscript dates Bowdoin's Collier's article to 11 August 1911; the project's direct OCR read identifies the issue as 19 August 1911. This should be resolved from the original newspaper issues, not silently corrected.

## Research decision
Do not promote the 1912 reply to primary-source tier until the Amherst Daily News original scan/microfilm is obtained and collated. The text documents a later account of Blair's claims, not an authenticated inscription or decipherment. No image or physical artifact was accessed.
