# Chappell's 1973 manuscript: secondary summary of Blair's 1912 reply

**Read date:** 2026-11-22  
**Source:** M. R. Chappell, *The True Story of Oak Island*, manuscript accompanied by a letter dated 20 September 1973 to Dan Blankenship.  
**PDF:** https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf  
**Relevant manuscript pages:** 44–46 (PDF text extraction lines 746–778).

## What the manuscript says
- Page 44 says Bowdoin's Collier's article appeared in an “August 11, 1911” issue. This conflicts with the Internet Archive issue record and OCR, which identify *Collier's*, Vol. 47, Issue 22 as 19 August 1911: https://archive.org/details/sim_colliers-the-national-weekly_1911-08-19_47_22
- The manuscript attributes a “categorical reply” by Frederick Blair to the Amherst Daily News of 23 February 1912.
- Pages 45–46 summarize six points: Blair's objections to Bowdoin's handling of the pit/cribbing; the Smith's Cove drains and water flow; the limits of Bowdoin's borings; the chain-link and parchment evidence; and the reported inscribed stone.
- Chappell's text is a retrospective manuscript summary. It is not the 1912 newspaper, a facsimile, or a verified verbatim transcript of Blair's article.

## Evidentiary boundary
This source establishes that a 1973 manuscript attributed a six-point response to Blair and gave a specific newspaper date. It does not establish that the exact 1912 issue survives, that the reply appeared exactly as summarized, or that Chappell's summary is accurate. Its Collier's date error is a concrete warning against treating it as a verbatim contemporary record.

## Research decision
Use the six-point outline only as an archival search aid. Obtain the original 23 February 1912 Amherst Daily News issue and compare Blair's wording with Chappell's summary and Bowdoin's six conclusions. Keep the 1911 Bowdoin OCR, 1973 manuscript summary and 1912 newspaper in separate source tiers.

No archive outreach, paid researcher, cryptanalysis or image analysis was performed.
