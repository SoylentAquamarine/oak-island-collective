# Amherst Daily News issue-access audit — 2026-11-23

## Question

Can the 23-February-1912 *Amherst Daily News* issue attributed with Frederick Blair's reply to H.L. Bowdoin be located and inspected?

## Directly checked

- UNB catalogue record: https://newspapers.lib.unb.ca/newspaper/daily-news-amherst-nova-scotia
- Catalogue holdings range: 2-November-1895 to 11-March-1914. The requested date falls within the listed range.
- Chappell 1973 manuscript: https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf — pages 44–46 summarize Blair's six-point reply; this is secondary, retrospective evidence.
- Modern secondary summary: https://thecurseofoakisland.com/hunt/captain-henry-bowdoin-and-the-old-gold-salvage-company — states that Blair answered in the *Daily News* on 23-February-1912 and that the Chappell manuscript preserves the rebuttal in summary form. This page is not the original issue and is not independent of the Chappell summary.
- Bowdoin's 1911 *Collier's* issue: https://archive.org/details/sim_colliers-the-national-weekly_1911-08-19_47_22 — the 19-August-1911 issue was read as OCR; its page images were not inspected.

## Result and limits

The UNB catalogue provides a plausible public microfilm access route covering the requested date. It does **not** establish that the exact issue survives, is legible, or contains Blair's reply. No original 1912 issue, image or faithful contemporary transcription was retrieved in this audit.

Do not treat the modern page and Chappell manuscript as two independent confirmations: the modern page explicitly attributes its summary to the manuscript. The August 11/18/19 date discrepancy in secondary references to Bowdoin's *Collier's* article remains separate and unresolved.

## Next action / authorization gate

Request issue-level confirmation and a lawful reproduction from UNB or another holding institution only after the user authorizes the inquiry or researcher expense. No contact was sent and no archive material was downloaded during this audit.
