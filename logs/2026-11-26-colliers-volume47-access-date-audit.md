# Collier's Volume 47 access and date-witness audit
Date: 2026-11-26.

## Sources checked
- Google Books, Collier's: Incorporating Features of the American Magazine, Volume 47 (1911), record GpBCAQAAIAAJ: https://books.google.com/books?id=GpBCAQAAIAAJ
- The Curse of Oak Island research archive's modern catalogue for Bowdoin's article, dated 19-Aug-1911: https://thecurseofoakisland.com/research/collier-s-solving-the-mystery-of-oak-island
- M. R. Chappell, The True Story of Oak Island (1973 manuscript transcription), which says the article appeared in the 11-Aug-1911 issue: https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/M%20R%20Chappell%20Manuscript%20Sept%201973.pdf
- K. Polsson's chronology, which lists 18-Aug-1911: https://kpolsson.com/oakisland/oak1909.htm

## Access result
Google Books identifies Volume 47 (1911) and labels the record full view. Its PDF download and plain-text endpoints both returned HTTP 403 in this session. No issue pages or Bowdoin article were read. The modern archive catalogue is not the original magazine.

## Date discrepancy
The three accessible witnesses disagree: Chappell's manuscript says 11 August; Polsson's chronology says 18 August; the modern archive catalogue says 19 August. These are later sources, not an authenticated original issue. No claim about the article's exact date or wording is promoted.

## Decision
The Volume 47 record is a volume-level lead only. Authenticate the exact issue and compare the original pages before treating Bowdoin's text as primary evidence. No archive contact, paid scan or expense was made or authorized.
