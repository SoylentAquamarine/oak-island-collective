## Directly read content
H. L. Bowdoin's first-person article says he examined the reported Money Pit stone at Creighton's bookbindery in Halifax during his 1909 expedition. He describes the rock as basalt, hard and fine-grained. He reports that the characters were “supposed to mean” “Ten feet below two million pounds lie buried.”
