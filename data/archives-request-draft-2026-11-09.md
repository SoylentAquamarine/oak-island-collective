# Updated draft Nova Scotia Archives request — 1863–1864 Oak Island correspondence

**Status:** draft for review; not sent. This revision supplements, rather than replaces, the immutable 2026-09-29 draft.

## New archival lead and its limits

A 2014 compilation by Les MacPhie, *Early Oak Island Documents, Part 1 of 3 — Early Newspaper Articles*, lists *The Colonist*'s “History of the Oak Island Enterprise” chapters for 2 January and 7 January 1864. Its introductory note says that the original handwritten letter dated **27 December 1863** is held by the Nova Scotia Archives and Record Management System and was likely written by Mr. Cooke, secretary to the Oak Island Association. The compilation is a transcription/secondary compilation, not the original manuscript or a direct archival finding aid. The original letter, its author, fonds, volume and item number remain unverified.

Source: [Les MacPhie compilation (May 2014), PDF](https://www.oakislandmystery.com/images/Les-MacPhie-Files/table-d/Early%20OI%20Docs%20Part%201%20of%203%20Early%20Newspaper%20Articles.pdf), especially its contents table and note 1. Existing project context: `logs/2026-09-28-stone-1864-colonist-account.md`.

The 27 December 1863 manuscript must **not** be conflated with the separate Hunter-Duvar letter dated 2 January 1864 or Cooke's reply dated 27 January 1864. The request below asks the archivist to establish whether and how these records relate.

## Draft request text

> **Subject: Research inquiry — 27 December 1863 Oak Island letter and January 1864 records**
>
> To Nova Scotia Archives reference staff,
>
> I am researching the documented history of the Oak Island Money Pit and am trying to identify several nineteenth-century records.
>
> A May 2014 compilation of early Oak Island newspaper transcriptions, *Early Oak Island Documents, Part 1 of 3 — Early Newspaper Articles* (compiled by Les MacPhie), lists *The Colonist*'s “History of the Oak Island Enterprise” chapters dated 2 January and 7 January 1864. Its introductory note states that an original handwritten letter dated 27 December 1863 is held by the Nova Scotia Archives and Record Management System and was likely written by Mr. Cooke, secretary to the Oak Island Association.
>
> Could you help identify the fonds, volume, item number or finding-aid entry for that **27 December 1863 handwritten letter**, and confirm whether it is held by your institution? If possible, could you also advise whether the original *Colonist* source material or related correspondence survives?
>
> Separately, secondary sources describe correspondence between John Hunter-Duvar and George Cooke: a Hunter-Duvar letter dated 2 January 1864 and Cooke's reply dated 27 January 1864 concerning a flagstone reportedly bearing characters. I do not assume these are the same documents as the 27 December 1863 letter. Do your holdings or finding aids identify either of those two January letters, or records of the historical society Hunter-Duvar represented?
>
> I would appreciate exact archival references, any access restrictions, and the applicable terms for consultation, citation, photography or reproduction. A pointer to another institution or collection would also be helpful if these records are not held by you.
>
> Thank you for your assistance.

## Research handling

- Treat the 2014 PDF as a locating lead, not as proof that the manuscript has been inspected or authenticated.
- Do not infer that the 27 December letter is the source of either January correspondence until the archive confirms its contents and relationship.
- Do not send this request from the project. An authorized human sender must submit it and record the archive's reply and permitted use.
