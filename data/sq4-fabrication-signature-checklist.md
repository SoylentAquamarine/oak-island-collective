## The cross-case pattern, visible only once assembled

- **Convenient promotional timing** and **commercial monetization** each recur as real matches
  across multiple independent comparators (Kensington + Oak Island's own 1894/1949 record).
- **The anonymous/misattributed insider chain** is the single most-corroborated signature in
  this entire checklist — it appears as a match or partial match under three different names
  across all three comparators (Beale's "traceable author," Kensington's "insider connection,"
  Dare Stones' "names absent from the record"), but all three point to **the same one
  underlying fact already on file**: this is one corroborated finding seen three ways, not
  three independent confirmations.
- **Every physical/textual examination signature is foreclosed by one single fact**: the
  original stone is lost. This produces several "not checkable" rows that are really one gap,
  not several.
- **The deepest remaining gap is structural, not a to-do list item**: the chain's most proximate
  anonymous layer (the schoolteacher, the "Irish instructor") has no name, so it cannot be
  background-checked the way Kempton and Snow were — this is not the same as "two down, more to
  go."
